# Mo Speech storyboards

Two six-panel storyboards for the case study, drawn in Figma as simple line art (rounded figures, hands, devices, props, 1.5px strokes). Rules for the drawings:

- People appear only in the scene. No people in app screens.
- No SymbolStix art anywhere. Every device screen is a flat placeholder labelled with the screen name, where the real screenshot goes later.
- Captions are plain, in Mo's voice, 10 words or fewer.

---

## Storyboard 1: A word, in the moment (the MVP, 2025)

The MVP is search only: by voice or typing. Tapping a symbol plays it and adds it to the top line. The play button plays the line full screen, one symbol at a time. There is no talker and no category navigation.

Panel 1
Scene: At the park, the child points at a dog while the parent stands beside them.
Screen: none
Caption: He points. I need the word, now.
Draw: Wide shot, park with a tree, a bench and a path. Child (small rounded figure) on the left, arm stretched out, index finger pointing right. Parent behind the child, hands empty, looking where the child points. A dog on a lead on the right, side view, tail up. No devices.

Panel 2
Scene: The parent takes out a phone and taps the microphone button to search.
Screen: MVP search, listening
Caption: Phone out, one tap on the mic.
Draw: Medium shot of the parent from the chest up, phone held in both hands at chest height, thumb on the mic button. Child at the parent's hip, still looking off-frame at the dog. Phone drawn portrait, tilted slightly towards the viewer. Placeholder on screen labelled "MVP search, listening": a search bar and a large mic circle, no symbols.

Panel 3
Scene: The parent says "dog" and the matching symbol comes up in the results.
Screen: MVP results
Caption: I say "dog" and the symbol appears.
Draw: Close-up of the phone held up in one hand, parent's face partly visible behind it with the mouth open, two small speech marks beside it. Phone portrait. Placeholder on screen labelled "MVP results": search bar with the word "dog" and one empty square tile marked "dog symbol". The child's head at the edge of the frame.

Panel 4
Scene: The parent taps play and the dog symbol fills the screen, with sound.
Screen: Full-screen playback
Caption: Play it, and it fills the screen.
Draw: Close-up of the phone, now landscape-filling the frame, thumb just leaving the play button at the bottom corner. Three short curved sound lines coming out of the phone. Placeholder on screen labelled "Full-screen playback": one large empty square marked "dog symbol", nothing else.

Panel 5
Scene: The child looks from the phone to the dog, the word and the thing joined up.
Screen: none
Caption: He looks from the phone to the dog.
Draw: Wide shot as in Panel 1. Child in profile on the left, head turned towards the dog on the right, with a faint dotted line from the phone (held low in the parent's hand, screen not shown) to the child's eyes and on to the dog. Parent smiling beside the child. Dog in the same pose as Panel 1.

Panel 6
Scene: The parent adds "big" and "dog" to the top line and plays the sequence.
Screen: Full-screen playback, second symbol
Caption: Then two words: "big" and "dog".
Draw: Over-the-shoulder shot of the parent holding the phone so the child can see it, child leaning in on the right. Phone portrait. Placeholder on screen labelled "Full-screen playback, 2 of 2": one large empty square marked "dog symbol", and a small row of two dots under it, the second filled. A small "big" and "dog" label pair floats beside the phone with an arrow between them. Dog small and blurred in the background.

## Storyboard 2: Show me where it lives (the full build)

The instructor starts modelling from the Model button on a category board (talker open or closed; on a phone it shows as an icon only). Every device on the student's profile follows. The student's tablet goes to Home, the page dims, the next target lights up with a pointer card and a step counter, and the student taps through Home, Categories, the category, the symbol. A tap anywhere else does nothing. The instructor's screen follows live. The student taps the last symbol and it speaks. Tones are a Max feature.

Panel 1
Scene: In the kitchen, the instructor opens the Food board on their phone, taps Model and picks "snack".
Screen: Choose a symbol to model (instructor's phone)
Caption: I pick "snack" and press Model.
Draw: Kitchen with a counter, a fruit bowl and a kettle. Instructor (adult rounded figure) standing at the counter on the left, phone in one hand, thumb on the screen. Student (smaller figure) sitting at the table on the right with a tablet lying flat in front of them, screen dark. Phone portrait. Placeholder on phone labelled "Choose a symbol to model": a title bar, a short list of empty rows with one row marked "snack" and ticked, and a "Start session" button at the bottom.

Panel 2
Scene: The student's tablet goes to Home, the page dims, and the Categories button lights up with a pointer.
Screen: Modelling, step 1 of 3 (student's tablet)
Caption: Their tablet dims. One button glows.
Draw: Close-up of the student's hands holding the tablet in landscape, index finger hovering over the left sidebar. Tablet drawn with a sidebar of four plain button shapes down the left. The page area drawn in a hatched, dimmed style, with the Categories button in the sidebar left clear and given a glow ring and a small pointer card beside it. Placeholder labelled "Modelling, Home: Categories lit, Step 1 of 3", with an exit button in the top corner.

Panel 3
Scene: The student taps Categories, then the Food tile.
Screen: Modelling, step 2 of 3 (student's tablet)
Caption: They tap Categories, then the Food tile.
Draw: Same close-up of the tablet and hands, finger now touching a tile in a grid of plain square tiles. Page dimmed with hatching, all tiles dimmed except one marked "Food", which has the glow ring and a pointer card. Placeholder labelled "Modelling, Categories: Food tile lit, Step 2 of 3". Small dotted arrow showing the finger moving from the sidebar to the tile.

Panel 4
Scene: The student finds "snack" on the Food board and taps it, while the instructor's phone shows the same step live.
Screen: Modelling, step 3 of 3 (student's tablet) and the instructor's phone
Caption: They find "snack" themselves. I watch it live.
Draw: Split composition. Left: the student's tablet in landscape on the table, finger on the lit tile marked "snack" among dimmed tiles, "Step 3 of 3" label. Right: the instructor's hand holding a phone, portrait, showing the same lit tile. Both placeholders carry the same label, "Modelling, Food board: snack lit, Step 3 of 3". Instructor leaning on the counter in the background, smiling, small speech marks coming off the tablet.

Panel 5
Scene: With the talker on, the student opens the dropdown, taps "I want" from Phrases, then taps "snack" on the board.
Screen: Talker with dropdown open, Phrases tab
Caption: Talker on. "I want" first, then "snack".
Draw: Student at the table, tablet in landscape upright on a stand, finger on a phrase card in a dropdown panel. Tablet drawn with a sentence strip across the top holding two empty blocks, a grey phrase box marked "I want" and a single-word block marked "snack", and a tab under the strip with the dropdown open below it. Dropdown has two small tabs, "Core words" and "Phrases", with Phrases selected and a few empty phrase cards. Placeholder label: "Talker, dropdown open on Phrases". Instructor's phone face-down on the table.

Panel 6
Scene: They press Play, the sentence plays full screen, and the student taps the excited face.
Screen: Play modal with tone faces
Caption: Play it, then tap the excited face.
Draw: Close-up of the tablet landscape, screen filled by the play modal: a blurred, dimmed backdrop, a panel in the middle with the two blocks "I want" and "snack" side by side, a glow ring on the block being spoken, and a row of three simple faces under the panel (angry, neutral, excited) with the right-hand excited face lit. Student's finger on that face. Placeholder label: "Play modal, tones". Instructor at the edge of the frame, laughing, hand on the student's shoulder.
