# Mo Speech storyboards

Two six-panel storyboards for the case study, drawn in Figma as simple line art (rounded figures, hands, devices, props, 1.5px strokes). Rules for the drawings:

- People appear only in the scene. No people in app screens.
- No SymbolStix art anywhere. Every device screen is a flat placeholder labelled with the screen name, where the real screenshot goes later.
- Captions are plain, in Mo's voice, 10 words or fewer.

---

## Storyboard 1: A word, in the moment (the MVP)

The MVP is search only: by voice or typing. Tapping a symbol plays it and adds it to the top line. The play button plays the line full screen, one symbol at a time. There is no talker and no category navigation. UI shapes on screens are generic placeholders.

Panel 1
Scene: At the park, the child points at a dog while the parent stands beside them.
Screen: none
Caption: He points. I need the word, now.
Draw: Wide shot, park with a tree, a bench and a path. Child (small rounded figure) on the left, arm stretched out, index finger pointing right. Parent behind the child, hands empty, looking where the child points. A dog on a lead on the right, side view, tail up. No devices.

Panel 2
Scene: The parent takes out a phone, taps the mic and says "dog".
Screen: MVP search, listening
Caption: Phone out, mic on, I say "dog".
Draw: Medium shot of the parent from the chest up, phone held in both hands at chest height, thumb on the mic button, mouth open with two small speech marks. Child at the parent's hip, looking off-frame at the dog. Phone drawn portrait. Placeholder on screen labelled "MVP search, listening": a search bar and a mic button, no symbols.

Panel 3
Scene: The dog symbol comes up and the parent taps it, so it plays and lands in the top line.
Screen: MVP results, top line with one block
Caption: The symbol appears. One tap plays it.
Draw: Close-up of the phone in one hand, portrait, thumb on a result tile, a few small sound lines beside the phone. Placeholder on screen labelled "MVP results": a search bar, a results grid with one empty tile marked "dog symbol", and a top line strip holding one empty block. The child's head at the edge of the frame.

Panel 4
Scene: The parent taps play and the dog symbol fills the screen, with sound.
Screen: Full-screen playback
Caption: Play it, and it fills the screen.
Draw: Closer framing of the phone, still portrait, thumb just leaving the play button, three short curved sound lines coming out of the phone. Placeholder on screen labelled "Full-screen playback": one large empty square marked "dog symbol", nothing else.

Panel 5
Scene: The child looks from the phone to the dog, the word and the thing joined up.
Screen: none
Caption: He looks from the phone to the dog.
Draw: Wide shot as in Panel 1. Child in profile on the left, head turned towards the dog on the right, with a faint dotted line from the phone (held low in the parent's hand, screen not shown) to the child's eyes and on to the dog. Parent smiling beside the child. Dog in the same pose as Panel 1.

Panel 6
Scene: The parent clears the top line, types "small", taps it, taps "dog" again so the top line reads "small dog", and plays the two words in order.
Screen: MVP results, top line with two blocks
Caption: Then "small dog", played in order.
Draw: Over-the-shoulder shot of the parent holding the phone so the child can see it, child leaning in on the right. Phone portrait. Placeholder on screen labelled "MVP search, top line": a search bar, a results grid with empty tiles marked "small symbol" and "dog symbol", a top line strip holding two empty blocks, and a play button. Parent's thumb on the play button. Dog small in the background.

## Storyboard 2: Show me where it lives (the full build)

The instructor starts modelling from the Model button on a category board (talker open or closed; on a phone it shows as an icon only). Every device on the student's profile follows. The student's tablet goes to Home, the page dims, the next target lights up with a pointer card and a step counter, and the student taps through Home, Categories, the category, the symbol. A tap anywhere else does nothing. The instructor's screen follows live. The student taps the last symbol and it speaks. Tones are a Max feature.

A simple one-word flow: no talker, no phrases. Drawn from the app screenshots in the Modelling-steps frame. Panel 1 is over the instructor's shoulder; panels 2 to 6 are over the student's shoulder at the kitchen table, tablet upright on a stand. Dimmed screens are a deep charcoal wash with cross-hatching; the one lit element stays paper-white with a muted orange outline, the only colour in the frame.

Panel 1
Scene: In the kitchen, the instructor picks "snack" on their phone and starts the session. The student waits at the table with their tablet.
Screen: Choose a symbol to model (instructor's phone)
Caption: I tap Model and pick "snack".
Draw: Over the instructor's shoulder, phone in the foreground showing "Choose a symbol to model" with "snack" selected and a "Start session" button. The student across the kitchen at the table, tablet in front of them.

Panel 2
Scene: The student's tablet goes to Home, the page dims, and the Categories button lights up with the snack hint card.
Screen: Modelling, step 1 of 3 (student's tablet)
Caption: Their tablet dims. One button glows.
Draw: Over the student's shoulder, finger reaching for the lit Categories button in the sidebar. "Step 1 of 3" pill at the top.

Panel 3
Scene: The student taps Categories, then the Food & Drink tile.
Screen: Modelling, step 2 of 3 (student's tablet)
Caption: They tap Categories, then Food & Drink.
Draw: Over the student's shoulder, finger on the lit Food & Drink tile among dimmed category tiles, snack hint card beside it. "Step 2 of 3".

Panel 4
Scene: The student finds "snack" on the Food & Drink board and taps it, while the instructor's phone shows the same step live.
Screen: Modelling, step 3 of 3 (student's tablet)
Caption: They find "snack" themselves. I watch it live.
Draw: Over the student's shoulder, finger on the lit "snack" tile among dimmed food tiles. "Step 3 of 3". The instructor soft in the background across the table, watching on their phone, smiling.

Panel 5
Scene: The screen undims. "Snack" is in the top line and the student plays it.
Screen: Food & Drink board with "snack" in the top line
Caption: "Snack" lands in the top line. They play it.
Draw: Over the student's shoulder, bright screen, "snack" card in the top line, finger on the speaker button, sound lines.

Panel 6
Scene: The screen dims again and the student rates the session.
Screen: Snack card with three faces
Caption: Then they tap the excited face.
Draw: Over the student's shoulder, dimmed screen, "snack" card in the middle with angry, plain and excited faces under it, finger on the lit excited face.
